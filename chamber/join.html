NP Membership Benefits

Designed for registered non-profit organizations in Ibadan with zero membership fee:

  • Listed in the Chamber member directory
  • Access to community networking forums
  • Invitations to public civic briefings

Bronze Membership Benefits

Ideal for emerging businesses building local connections:

  • Full member directory profile
  • Standard admission to monthly business mixers
  • Access to foundational SME workshops

Silver Membership Benefits

Built for growing enterprises seeking greater reach and training:

  • Eligibility for homepage Business Spotlight rotation
  • Discounted rates on seminars and trade fair booths
  • Priority access to specialized training sessions

Gold Membership Benefits

Comprehensive executive package for established leaders:

  • Featured homepage Business Spotlight eligibility
  • Complimentary or priority passes to flagship events
  • Direct policy roundtable participation and leadership workshops
